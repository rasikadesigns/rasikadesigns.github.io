Case Study 01

Nexus SaaS Dashboard

Nexus was drowning in its own complexity — a sprawling analytics platform with 200+ features, a 6-week onboarding curve, and a 42% churn rate. My mission: rebuild the entire experience from the ground up, making power users faster and novices instantly productive.

38%
Churn reduction
40%
Task completion ↑
4.9★
User satisfaction
12w
Discovery to ship

Design Process

01
Discover
30 user interviews, heatmap analysis, session recordings across 3 user segments.
02
Define
Identified 3 critical pain points: cognitive overload, poor navigation, and data blindness.
03
Design
Progressive disclosure system, unified component library (280 components), 4 iteration rounds.
04
Deliver
Full handoff docs, motion specs, developer tokens, and A/B test frameworks.

Key Design Decisions

Replaced flat navigation with a context-aware sidebar that surfaces relevant tools based on the user's active workflow. Introduced "Focus Mode" that collapses secondary panels, reducing visible UI elements by 60% during analysis tasks. Built an adaptive dashboard that learns from user behavior — surfacing the 5 most-used widgets on the home screen.

Case Study 02

Bloom Finance Mobile App

Bloom wanted to make personal finance feel approachable for 18–28 year olds who find traditional banking apps intimidating. Starting from zero, I designed a full iOS & Android experience that launched with a 4.8★ rating and 50,000 downloads in 3 months.

4.8★
App Store rating
50K
Downloads (month 3)
68%
Day-7 retention

Design Process

01
Research
Diary studies with 20 Gen Z participants tracking real financial stress moments.
02
Strategy
Defined "calm design" principles — no red numbers, positive framing, celebration moments.
03
Design
6 prototype iterations, 3 usability rounds, custom illustration system, micro-animation library.
04
Launch
Phased rollout, feature flags for A/B testing onboarding flows, live NPS monitoring.

Key Design Decisions

Designed a "financial mood" onboarding that segments users in 3 taps instead of 20-question forms. Built a spending visualization using organic blob shapes instead of harsh pie charts — users reported feeling "less judged" by their data. Created a custom haptic feedback pattern for savings milestones that users described as "weirdly satisfying."

Case Study 03

Orbit Analytics Platform

Orbit was a Series A startup with an engineering-led team and no design culture. I joined as the sole designer and shipped their entire product experience in 16 weeks — from initial discovery through a robust design system that let the team scale independently after I left.

3mo
Ahead of schedule
280
Design system components
2×
Team velocity increase
$2M
ARR at launch

Design Process

01
Audit
Competitive analysis of 12 analytics tools, stakeholder interviews, technical constraint mapping.
02
Foundation
Design tokens, typography scale, color system, and spacing grid — all built in week 2.
03
Build
Parallel design-dev workflow using Figma variables linked directly to the codebase.
04
Scale
Documented every component with usage guidelines so the team could extend the system themselves.

Key Design Decisions

Introduced Figma Variables linked directly to CSS custom properties, eliminating the design-dev token translation layer entirely. Built a "complexity dial" — users switch between Simplified, Standard, and Expert view without changing their underlying data. Shipped a custom chart library with 24 visualization types, all accessible-first by default.
